Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how RAC Forge Pvt. Ltd. (https://www.racforge.com) accesses, uses, stores, protects, retains, deletes, and discloses information when you use the RAC Forge Pvt. Ltd. training portal hosted at https://training.racforge.com, including user data received through Google OAuth 2.0 and Firebase Authentication.

1. Google User Data We Access

When you sign in with your Google Account, the RAC Forge Pvt. Ltd. training portal requests only standard, non-sensitive Google sign-in identity scopes via Firebase Authentication:

OAuth Scope Google User Data Accessed Purpose Within the Application
openid Unique Google / Firebase user identifier (UID) Securely authenticate your session and link your training progress records to your account.
email Verified Google email address and email verification status Verify whether your email address is authorized by a RAC Forge Pvt. Ltd. administrator to access assigned training modules.
profile Display name and profile picture URL (when present) Display your identity inside the training workspace and identify learners on compliance completion reports.

No Sensitive or Restricted Google API Scopes: The RAC Forge Pvt. Ltd. training portal does not request, access, read, or modify your Gmail messages, personal Google Drive files, Google Calendar events, Google Contacts, Google Photos, or any other Google Workspace user data. Training videos embedded in the portal are administrator-supplied links rendered in a standard browser frame and do not access the learner's personal Google Drive account via OAuth.

2. Additional Portal Activity Data We Collect

Once an authorized user signs in and agrees to this Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service, we store application-generated learning records required to operate the training platform:

  • First-time legal policy acceptance timestamp and policy version identifier.
  • Assigned training track permissions (configured by RAC Forge Pvt. Ltd. administrators).
  • Module completion status, sequential unlock state, questionnaire scores, attempt counts, and active watch duration.

3. How We Use Google User Data

RAC Forge Pvt. Ltd. uses Google user data strictly to provide and maintain the core functionality of the RAC Forge Pvt. Ltd. training portal:

  • Authenticating authorized personnel and preventing unauthorized access to internal training content.
  • Determining which training curriculum tracks an authenticated email address is authorized to view.
  • Persisting learner progress, quiz attempts, and pass status across sessions.
  • Generating internal training completion and compliance reports for authorized RAC Forge Pvt. Ltd. administrators.

We do not use Google user data for targeted advertising, behavioral profiling, data brokerage, marketing campaigns, creditworthiness assessments, or training generalized artificial intelligence (AI) or machine learning (ML) models.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy & Limited Use Disclosure

Limited Use Compliance: RAC Forge Pvt. Ltd. training portal's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Sharing, Transfer, and Disclosure of Google User Data

RAC Forge Pvt. Ltd. does not sell, rent, trade, or transfer Google user data to third parties for advertising, analytics resale, or any purpose unrelated to operating the training portal. Access to stored data is strictly limited to:

  • Cloud Infrastructure Sub-processor (Google Cloud / Firebase): Authentication tokens, user profile metadata, and training progress records are processed and stored on Google Cloud Platform (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore) solely to host and operate the portal.
  • Authorized RAC Forge Pvt. Ltd. Administrators: Designated company administrators can view learner emails, display names, and training completion metrics solely to manage team onboarding and internal compliance. Regular learners cannot view any other user's email address or records.
  • Legal Compliance: Information may be disclosed only if strictly required by applicable law, regulation, subpoena, or enforceable governmental request.

6. Data Protection & Security Mechanisms

RAC Forge Pvt. Ltd. implements technical and organizational safeguards to protect Google user data and training records against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure:

  • Encryption in Transit: All connections to https://training.racforge.com and Firebase services are encrypted using HTTPS / TLS 1.2+.
  • Encryption at Rest: All data stored in Google Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication is encrypted at rest using Google-managed AES-256 encryption.
  • Server-Enforced Access Controls: Cloud Firestore Security Rules enforce a default-deny posture, require verified Google email claims (email_verified == true), isolate personal user records strictly to the record owner and authorized administrators, and reject unauthorized or malformed writes.
  • Zero Password Storage: Authentication is delegated exclusively to Google OAuth 2.0 via Firebase Authentication; RAC Forge Pvt. Ltd. never sees, collects, or stores your Google password.
  • Least-Privilege Administration: Administrative privileges are restricted to explicitly designated RAC Forge Pvt. Ltd. administrator accounts.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Retention Period: We retain your Google identity profile (email, display name, profile URL) and training completion records only for as long as your account remains authorized in the RAC Forge Pvt. Ltd. training portal, and for up to 12 months following access revocation or offboarding when needed for internal regulatory compliance auditing, unless you request earlier deletion.

How to Revoke Access or Request Data Deletion:

  • Revoke Google Account Access: You may revoke the RAC Forge Pvt. Ltd. training portal's access to your Google Account at any time via https://myaccount.google.com/permissions .
  • Request Permanent Data Deletion or Export: Email sankhyayan@gmail.com or racforge@gmail.com with the subject line “Data Deletion Request — RAC Forge Pvt. Ltd. training portal” from your registered email address. Verified deletion requests are completed within 15 business days, permanently removing your user profile, team allowlist entry, module progress, and engagement records from Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy & User Notification

We keep this Privacy Policy current and review it whenever our data practices change. If RAC Forge Pvt. Ltd. makes any material change to how the RAC Forge Pvt. Ltd. training portal accesses, uses, stores, or shares Google user data, we will update the Effective Date and Policy Version above, post a prominent in-portal notification requiring re-acknowledgement on your next sign-in, and notify authorized users via email before the changes take effect.
